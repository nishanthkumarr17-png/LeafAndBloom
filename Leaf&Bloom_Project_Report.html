ABSTRACT

Leaf & Bloom is a modern web-based plant shopping platform designed to make plant discovery, care guidance, and community participation simple and engaging. Traditional online shopping platforms often focus only on product listing and order placement, but plant buyers usually need more support, especially beginners who may not know how to care for different plant types. This project addresses that gap by combining a plant catalog, personalized plant guidance, a beginner care guide, wishlist management, order handling, user authentication, and a community space where plant lovers can share their journey and experiences.

The frontend of the system is developed using React and Vite to provide a fast and responsive user experience, while the backend uses Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB to manage order data and wishlist-related information. The system includes category browsing, room-based discovery, plant detail pages, reviews, chatbot-style assistance, community posts with images, and structured plant care information. Leaf & Bloom is designed not only as an e-commerce solution but also as a friendly digital companion for plant lovers.

1. INTRODUCTION

Indoor plants have become an important part of modern lifestyles because they improve aesthetics, support well-being, and create a closer connection with nature. However, many buyers struggle to choose the right plant, understand its care requirements, and maintain it successfully after purchase. General e-commerce websites usually do not offer detailed plant-specific guidance or community-based support.

Leaf & Bloom is developed as a specialized online plant store that offers a complete experience around plant shopping and care. It helps users browse plants based on category, room suitability, lighting conditions, beginner friendliness, and lifestyle preferences. In addition to shopping, the platform provides care guidance, a community space, and a context-aware plant assistant to make the experience more helpful and personalized.

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Existing shopping platforms treat plants like ordinary products and often fail to support customers with practical plant-care knowledge. Users may purchase plants without understanding light needs, watering frequency, humidity, pet safety, or maintenance difficulty. This leads to poor plant survival, customer dissatisfaction, and a low level of confidence among beginners. There is a need for a dedicated digital platform that combines plant shopping with educational guidance and user interaction.

3. OBJECTIVES

  • To design a dedicated online platform for buying indoor and lifestyle plants.
  • To provide structured plant-specific guidance for beginners and regular plant owners.
  • To implement shopping features such as catalog browsing, wishlist, checkout, and order history.
  • To add a plant community where users can share experiences, images, and joy.
  • To offer a plant assistant that responds to care questions using plant-specific context.
  • To build an interface that is visually attractive, simple to navigate, and mobile friendly.

4. EXISTING SYSTEM

In the existing system, users normally rely on generic e-commerce platforms or scattered social media pages to buy plants. Product information is often limited to names, prices, and a few images. Plant care support is inconsistent, community interaction is disconnected from the buying process, and beginner guidance is rarely integrated into the shopping flow. As a result, buyers often feel uncertain before and after purchase.

5. PROPOSED SYSTEM

The proposed system, Leaf & Bloom, integrates plant shopping and support into one platform. It includes a rich product catalog, category-based navigation, room-based browsing, reviews, wishlist management, checkout, and order management. It also includes a care guide, a plant assistant for customized suggestions, and a community page for shared learning and engagement. This makes the platform more useful than a standard storefront because it supports both plant selection and plant success after purchase.

7. SYSTEM DESIGN AND ARCHITECTURE

The system follows a client-server architecture. The React frontend handles user interaction, rendering, filtering, and browsing experiences. The Express backend handles API requests and persists order and wishlist data in MongoDB. Static and structured plant data is used to enrich the user experience with care guidance and category browsing.

Layer Description
Presentation Layer React-based UI for catalog, plant details, login, wishlist, checkout, care guide, and community.
Application Layer Business logic for filtering, sorting, review handling, assistant responses, and user interaction flows.
API Layer Express routes for orders and wishlist management.
Data Layer MongoDB collections for order data and wishlist items, plus structured plant data for product information.

8. MODULES DESCRIPTION

8.1 Authentication Module

This module provides Sign In and Sign Up pages for user entry into the system. It creates the basic onboarding flow needed for a personalized shopping experience.

8.2 Plant Catalog Module

This module displays all plant products with filters such as size, light requirement, price range, difficulty, and category. It supports search, sorting, plant collection counters, and category shortcuts such as low light, palms, pet friendly, flowering plants, gift sets, succulents, and trailing plants.

8.3 Plant Details Module

Each plant has a dedicated page that presents detailed care information including watering, humidity, pet safety, placement, soil type, temperature, and fertilizer needs. This module also supports related browsing and plant reviews.

8.4 Wishlist Module

Users can save plants they are interested in and revisit them later. This helps improve decision making and encourages return visits to the platform.

8.5 Checkout and Orders Module

This module handles purchasing flow and stores order information through the backend. It allows users to place orders and view their order history.

8.6 Care Guide Module

This module gives beginner-friendly advice on plant maintenance, including watering routines, light conditions, common issues, and recovery tips for plant stress.

8.7 Plant Assistant Module

A chatbot-style assistant helps users understand how to care for specific plants. It responds with customized guidance based on the active plant and structured plant data.

8.8 Community Module

Users can share their plant journeys, joy, experiences, and images through a community feed. This improves engagement and creates a sense of belonging for plant lovers.

9. DATABASE DESIGN

MongoDB is used as the backend database for order placement and wishlist information. The data model is simple, flexible, and suitable for storing product-related user actions.

Collection Sample Fields
orders customerName, phone, address, plantId, plantName, quantity, totalAmount, createdAt
wishlist-items plantId, name, image, price, category, light, size

Additional structured plant data is maintained in the frontend to support care details, plant categories, and assistant responses.
